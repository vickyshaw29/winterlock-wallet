    The stuck payment's first try gets only 4 seconds, less than one 6-second block of the devnet, so it misses its block and expires: its ticket is burned. Then one retry goes out on a fresh lane with the normal deadline (90 s). Both offer the market's fee. A low fee would not make it stuck: on this devnet a fee below the 7-wei base fee is refused at admission, and any fee above it is included. The expired leaf is never re-signed and never fee-bumped.
